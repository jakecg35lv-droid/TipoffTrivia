Privacy Policy

Last updated November 18, 2026

This policy explains what Tipoff Trivia (trivia.tipofffantasy.com), operated by Tipoff LLC, collects and why. Short version: we collect as little as we can, and we don't sell your data.

If you play without an account

  • On your device: your Daily progress, streak, settings, and league choice are saved in your browser's local storage. That data stays on your device.
  • Analytics: we use PostHog to count visits and see which features people use (for example, games started, packs picked, buttons clicked). This includes device and browser type, approximate location from your IP address, and pages viewed. It doesn't include your name.

If you create an account

  • Email and password, handled by Google Firebase Authentication. We never see your password.
  • Game records: whether you've played today's Daily and your score, so the one-per-day limit works across devices.
  • Purchase status: whether Unlimited is active on your account.

If you buy Unlimited

Checkout is handled by Stripe. Stripe collects your payment details under its own privacy policy. We receive the purchase email, amount, date, and a Stripe reference ID, never your full card number. We keep purchase records as long as the law requires for tax and accounting.

How we use it

  • To run the Game, unlock what you paid for, and enforce one Daily per day
  • To answer support requests and process refunds
  • To understand which features and questions work, and improve them

We don't sell or rent personal information, and we don't run third-party ads.

Who we share with

Only the service providers that run the Game: Google Firebase (accounts and database), Stripe (payments), PostHog (analytics), and GitHub Pages (hosting). We'll also share data if the law requires it.

Your choices

  • Clear your browser storage to erase local game data.
  • Email admin@tipofffantasy.com to get a copy of your account data or to delete your account. Deleting your account removes Unlimited access.

Children

Tipoff Trivia isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal information. If you think a child under 13 has created an account, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
